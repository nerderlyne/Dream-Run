# Active-time dream transitions

The initial photo begins changing at 20 active seconds. Every following start is independently seeded 30–90 seconds after the previous start. Pauses do not advance active time; speed and stumbling do not delay the cadence. The schedule is reconstructed from the dream identity and active seconds and uses its own RNG stream.

Overlaps last 80% of that interval, capped at 48 seconds (24–48 seconds). One third linger around the mixed middle of the blend. This gives the superimposed reality substantial screen time, then allows a settled interval before the next change. The outgoing photo stays behind the incoming one; there is no fade through black. A next-photo preload and the existing three-photo cache are retained. Missing imagery preserves the current photo until replacement is available.

Mirror/drop transitions retain their 1.8-second buffered change. Collage props and atmospheric fragments continue their independent staggered evolution. Three-hour void and Lucky Dream visibility rules remain separate.
