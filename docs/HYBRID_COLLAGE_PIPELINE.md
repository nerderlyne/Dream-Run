# Current collage pipeline

`DreamCollageKit.swift` combines 22 owner-photo transparent objects from `DreamOwnerObjectKit.swift` with 122 owner-curated full-frame background plates. The earlier generated collage cutouts, generated backdrops and fictional repost cards have been removed. Source and curation records for the latest 59 downloads are in `data/dream_objects_intake.json`; older plate provenance is in `data/dream_plates.json`. The supplied mood references remain reference-only.

`HybridDreamLayers` holds 20 semantic object cards, three atmospheric cards and two camera-relative sky cards. Cards follow the runner camera with perspective and parallax. Texture loading is asynchronous and cached; plate textures use a bounded three-photo working set. Cards are sorted back to front, cast no shadows and cannot collide with gameplay. Ordinary replacements fade at staggered distance cells. Sky transitions use a 24-active-second dissolve, accelerated to 1.8 seconds for mirrors and safe drops. Three-hour visual stripping and rebuilding remain nonterminal.

The five seeded `DreamVignette` compositions each use four different owner-photo cutouts. One of each appears in a shuffled five-cell cycle. Cultural apparition objects occur only in these vignettes. Ordinary scenery uses a shuffled deck that visits eligible owner cutouts before repeating. `DreamAtmosphere` reuses the owner-photo architecture, trees, cloud, jellyfish and ribbon as translucent large forms; it adds no world families or texture resources. The entity pool remains 25 cards.

`DreamScaleComposition` permits one scene-wide visual anomaly at slot zero: 60% none, 15% miniature, 19% oversized, 5% monumental and 1% absurd. It selects a horse, moon/planet, house, tree or arch from the owner-photo catalog. Supporting objects stay smaller, can sit below the route and fade before exceeding the size budget. Gameplay luck is independent. The 42 procedural world-asset families and pig #42 remain unchanged.

The track, hazards and articulated straw looper remain native geometry. The photo cards are background-only. Reduced Motion freezes their subtle drift; Low Power keeps only one atmospheric layer. The next visual gate is to review scene combinations on the simulator and retune scale and opacity where real photos fight the path.
