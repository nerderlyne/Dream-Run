# Composition lessons from app_target_01

The owner identified `references/app_target_01.jpeg` as an unusually successful opening. Its seed is unknown, so this is a reading of the visible frame, not a reconstruction of that run or proof of why every viewer would prefer it.

The ruined arch is cropped beyond the upper and left frame. It suggests architecture continuing outside the image, rather than a collectible object displayed in full. Its opening and the visible sky preserve negative space. Stone texture and the purple sky overlap without either vanishing. The tiny moon, houses and distant vegetation establish a second scale. The subdued green track provides a large quiet foreground against the textured violet background; the pink balloons remain distinct.

The useful relationship is strong form + permeable texture + small context + quiet route. The exact arch, cosmos image and green track are not a template to repeat.

## Generator changes

Four seeded atmospheric arrangements vary the spatial relationship: apertures, overhead canopies, submerged forms and suspended architecture. Each uses several eligible images. The primary atmosphere is stronger; a secondary form occupies the opposite side at lower opacity; a faint cloud/fog layer preserves superposition. All three remain large. This is separate from the existing rare semantic giant event and does not reduce ordinary scenery scale diversity.

The primary image uses 0.32 base opacity, the secondary 0.12, and the veil 0.09, before existing fades and route-overlap attenuation. Cropping and elevation depend on the arrangement. This gives the sky room to remain legible rather than accumulating three equally emphatic layers.

Track surface selection now responds to composition: 65% solid / 20% stripes / 15% checker for the more architectural or dense silhouettes; 40% / 20% / 40% for submerged arrangements. This is a probability, not a guarantee or a ban on patterned tracks. Selection is stable within each 192 m surface segment.

Atmospheric positions are sampled once per card lifetime. Their existing unequal lifetimes and fades remain; a new arrangement enters one card at a time. The system does not recolor or rebuild the visible world at a composition boundary. Existing void density, semantic scale events, underfoot framing, gameplay and the 42-family registry remain in effect. No extra textures or renderer cards are added.

## Evaluation limits

The review gallery captures consecutive seeds 0–7 in isolated DEBUG runs at 60 m, without forced sky, track pattern, semantic scale event or palette. These are repeatable visual samples, not evidence of a measured improvement in player preference. Tests check determinism, variety, relative visual emphasis, surface stability and existing renderer bounds. They cannot judge beauty.

Remaining limitations: no image-content-aware saliency or color matching, no per-pixel corridor mask, and no guarantee that a chosen primary silhouette remains in view on every route bend. Physical-device GPU performance and moving-scene preference need playtesting. The screenshot's exact colors are deliberately not imposed on every seed.

Visual review of the final eight captures: seeds 0–2 happen to share the orchid primary against different skies; this is retained in the gallery rather than hiding repetition. Seed 3 begins in the intended black void. Seeds 4–5 have weaker pale atmospheric silhouettes; seed 6 has a cropped jellyfish against warm clouds. These expose the remaining color/contrast-selection limitation. The gallery is evidence for the implemented hierarchy and its limitations, not a claim that every seed matches the owner's preferred frame.
